The portfolio

Different workflows required different products.

01

Executive planning

Connected demand, staffing, and growth assumptions in a planning surface leaders used to allocate resources.

Hover to inspect
Decision supported: where staffing risk required intervention first.
02

Clinical operations

Translated source-system data into repeatable views of operating performance.

Trust mechanism: definitions traced back to source logic.
03

People Growth

Made vacancies, turnover, starts, and hiring pressure legible across facilities.

Decision supported: hire, move, or retain talent.
04

Automated reporting

Removed recurring manual preparation from weekly reporting cycles.

Outcome: 10 to 15 hours of weekly work removed.
05

Data quality

Defined freshness, accuracy, access, and audit expectations before release.

Guardrail: a fast answer never outranked a trustworthy answer.
06

Adoption system

Paired launches with weekly user feedback, training, and usage signals so publication did not become the finish line.

90%+
Success included repeated decision use, not dashboard views alone.

Operating model

Question → definition → product → adoption → evidence

I moved between clinical leaders, field teams, engineering, analytics, finance, and compliance. The product loop kept technical constraints connected to the decision each release needed to improve.
