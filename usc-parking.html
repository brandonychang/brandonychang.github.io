01

One decision, scattered evidence

A parking decision depends on more than distance. Traffic instructions, eligibility rules, route conditions, maps, the destination, and a driver’s priorities live in separate systems. Traditional search returns fragments and leaves the user to reconcile them while traveling.

This prototype reframes search as decision support. The system retrieves relevant evidence, scores alternatives under current trip conditions, and shows why the recommendation changed.

Retrieval before recommendation

  1. 01

    Interpret the trip

    Capture destination, timing, origin, and the user’s optimization priority.

  2. 02

    Retrieve policy context

    Use FAISS to find passages that apply to the destination and situation.

  3. 03

    Join route signals

    Combine retrieved context with map relationships and condition freshness.

  4. 04

    Rank with an explanation

    Return a primary choice, visible tradeoffs, alternatives, and the evidence behind the score.

04

Product limits

Freshness

Conditions expire

Timestamp every live signal and lower confidence when a source becomes stale.

Uncertainty

Availability is not guaranteed

Frame the answer as a recommendation and keep a viable alternative visible.

Trust

Rules need provenance

Separate retrieved facts from model synthesis and link restrictions to source passages.

Evaluation

Test the decision

Measure relevance, route usefulness, freshness, override rate, and whether users understand the rationale.
