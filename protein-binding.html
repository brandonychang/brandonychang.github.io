01

The research question

Drug discovery depends on understanding how a candidate molecule interacts with a protein target. The source data arrives as atom coordinates, chemical attributes, and inconsistent structural records. A model needs a representation that preserves relationships, not a flat list of coordinates.

I worked on deep-learning and graph-based approaches that learn interaction patterns from molecular and protein datasets, with equal attention to the pipeline that prepares the data and the tooling that reveals when an experiment is unreliable.

02

From structure to graph

  1. 01

    Parse

    Read molecular records with Python and RDKit, retaining the attributes needed for modeling.

  2. 02

    Represent

    Treat atoms as nodes and chemical or spatial relationships as edges.

  3. 03

    Train

    Use PyTorch graph and deep-learning models to learn latent interaction patterns.

  4. 04

    Evaluate

    Track model performance, experiment lineage, data freshness, and pipeline health across research stages.

03

Feature exploration

Scientific feature discovery creates a second bottleneck. Relevant signals live across dense papers and structural literature, while model experiments move faster than a manual review cycle.

I integrated LLM-based analysis tools to accelerate early feature exploration. The tools helped surface candidate signals for scientific review, which shortened iteration time by about 40%. The model still required domain validation. The language model served as an exploration aid, not an authority.

Product principleUse AI to narrow the search space. Keep scientific judgment in the loop.
04

Evaluation as a product surface

Model quality

Prediction error

Measure the distance between predicted binding behavior and available experimental labels.

Scientific risk

False positives

Track confident predictions that do not hold up against experimental evidence.

System health

Data and pipeline checks

Catch corrupted or incomplete structural records before they contaminate training.

Delivery

Experiment visibility

Use Git-based workflows and reporting views so model changes remain traceable.

05

What the work taught me

Model architecture was only one layer of the product. Representation choices, corrupted inputs, experiment tracking, and evaluation design determined whether a research result deserved attention.

The work strengthened a principle I now carry into analytics products. A sophisticated model is useful only when users understand its inputs, its limits, and the evidence behind its output.
