Side project / Customer support AI

GenAIChatbot

A support chatbot that answers from approved company information, completes allowed tasks, and sends uncertain questions to a person.

Role
Product scoping · requirements · prototype delivery
Scope
Company documents · answer search · approved actions · web integration
Measures
Useful answers · source quality · response speed · handoffs

Project goal

Reduce repeat support work without letting the assistant invent policy.

The intended user was someone with a common account or product question. The assistant needed to find the relevant approved document, answer with a visible source, complete only explicitly supported actions, and hand the conversation to a person when the evidence was weak.

My work
Defined the use case, requirements, retrieval flow, action boundaries, escalation states, and evaluation measures.
Deliverable
A working product concept and PRD-ready interaction model for grounded answers, supported actions, and human handoffs.

Product promise

Answer from the company’s knowledge. Take only supported actions. Escalate when the evidence runs out.

01

Scope the job, not the technology

The prototype focused on repeatable support questions with documented answers. It did not try to act like a general assistant. This boundary shaped the knowledge base, the supported tools, and the moments where the conversation should move to a person.

You

Why did my account access expire?

Assistant

I found the access policy and renewal steps in the approved help center. Here is the relevant guidance and its source.

System

Source attached · confidence checked · escalation available

02

The retrieval loop

01IngestFAQs and support docs
02ChunkPreserve useful context
03RetrieveRank relevant passages
04GenerateAnswer from evidence
05Act or hand offUse tools within bounds

Chunk size creates a product tradeoff. Large chunks preserve context but dilute retrieval precision. Small chunks improve specificity but split related instructions. The requirements therefore connect chunking decisions with retrieval tests, not a fixed technical recipe.

03

Three response paths

Known answer

Respond with a source

Retrieve relevant approved content and ground the response in the passage.

Supported action

Call a tool

Collect required information, confirm intent, then create a ticket or run an allowed workflow.

Insufficient evidence

Escalate clearly

Avoid guessing. Explain the limit and pass context to a human support path.

04

Evaluation before expansion

SignalQuestionFailure to catch
Retrieval relevanceDid the system find the right passage?Fluent answer from the wrong document
Supported by sourcesDoes every claim follow the evidence?Invented policy or unsupported step
Task qualityDid the response resolve the support job?Accurate but unusable guidance
LatencyDid the answer arrive at conversational speed?A correct experience users abandon
EscalationDid uncertainty trigger the right handoff?False confidence
05

What made the product useful

The chatbot needed reliable company documents, clear limits, and a simple handoff to a person. The AI model was only one part of the experience.

I measured whether answers were useful, supported by the right source, and fast enough for a real support conversation.
