The actual portfolio

Hover or focus each release to see the product outcome.

01 / Knowledge

Glean AI Knowledge Assistant

Rebuilt trust in an AI assistant that helped clinic staff search thousands of policy and procedure pages.

Reliability before scale
87% daily active use in three pilot clinics
Cleaned the source library, categorized failure modes, added safe fallback behavior, and shipped prebuilt prompts around common policy questions.
02 / Safety

Safety Action Dashboard

Turned survey scores into a meeting-ready path from signal to root cause to action.

Adoption rose from 35% to more than 80%
Protected anonymity, grouped themes, and embedded action plans so the tool supported clinics instead of ranking them.
03 / People

PCT Retention Journey

Used the six-to-nine-month quit spike to redesign the technician career journey.

Pilot retention length increased 40%
Connected stay bonuses, training milestones, a visible career ladder, and manager coaching aids.
04 / Reporting

Pyramid Report Modernization

Replaced stale, manually assembled clinical reporting with one shared operating view.

Reporting time fell 80%; 15 hours saved weekly
Mapped 15 manual handoffs, retired low-value metrics, standardized definitions, automated ETL, and added contextual write-back.
05 / Quality

Clinical Requirements Heatmap

Converted a manual comparison across treatment requirements into a visual quality-control refresh.

Four hours became five seconds
Unpivoted source files in Power Query, converted qualitative rules to numeric logic, and highlighted missing requirements immediately.
06 / Operations

Healthcare Analytics Request Tracker

Turned a shared Outlook inbox into a structured request system without asking staff to learn a new intake process.

EMAILCLASSIFYSLA
About 250 requests structured across 40+ fields
Automated classification, thread deduplication, ownership, business-day SLAs, resolution tracking, and an offline leadership dashboard.

What connects the six

Each launch made a difficult behavior easier.

01

Find the real friction

Trust, fear of punishment, career ambiguity, stale data, manual comparison, and inbox overload were different surface problems. Each required a distinct product response.

02

Define the operating action

I designed around what a nurse, field leader, manager, or analyst needed to do next, then selected the metric and interface that supported that action.

03

Measure workflow change

Adoption, time saved, retention, reporting speed, and reliable usage mattered more than shipping a dashboard or model alone.
