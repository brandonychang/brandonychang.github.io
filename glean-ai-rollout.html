DaVita / Enterprise AI adoption

Access is not adoption.

The rollout focused on the gap between receiving an AI search tool and trusting the answer enough to use it again inside a real healthcare workflow.

Four barriers to trust

Hover to see the problem and the response.

01

Answer quality

People saw answers that were not consistent enough to trust.

Response: track repeated problems and test every fix with real users.
02

Clear sources

People needed to see where each answer came from.

Response: treat an answer without a reliable source as a product failure.
03

Missing information

Useful documents lived in separate systems, so search could miss them.

Response: connect the most trusted sources for each user group first.
04

Fit with daily work

Opening a separate tool added another step to common tasks.

Response: place AI search closer to the browser and support tools people already used.

Access did not create confidence.

Fewer than one in five invited users tried the assistant. Feedback identified privacy anxiety and confidently wrong answers as the largest barriers.

  • 40% of negative feedback cited privacy concerns
  • 35% cited confidently wrong answers
  • The team treated refusal as product evidence, not user resistance
