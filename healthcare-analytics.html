(01) — Case study

From question to adoption

Frame the operating problem

Partnered with clinical, finance, field, and technical stakeholders to define the decision each product needed to support. Converted ambiguous requests into measurable requirements, source definitions, and release priorities.

Build trustworthy systems

Traced metrics to source systems, designed repeatable transformations, and tested edge cases before release. The portfolio included dashboards, planning tools, operational reporting, and workflow products.

Ship and drive adoption

Stayed involved through release, user feedback, training, and iteration. This product-ownership approach connected technical delivery with daily workflow change rather than treating publication as the finish line.
