Side project · Customer support AI

GenAI Chatbot Prototype

A retrieval-augmented customer-support chatbot designed to answer from approved company knowledge, complete supported actions, and escalate cases when the available evidence is insufficient.

Role

Product scoping and delivery

Architecture

Retrieval-augmented generation

Methods

Chunking, embeddings, retrieval, tools, web integration

(01) — Case study

Ground every answer in evidence

Define the support job

Scoped the product around repeatable customer questions, knowledge retrieval, and clear escalation boundaries. The goal was a useful help-center experience rather than an unrestricted general chatbot.

Prepare the knowledge base

Defined document ingestion and chunking requirements for FAQs, help articles, and troubleshooting guides. The design balanced semantic context with chunks small enough for precise retrieval.

Retrieve before generating

Specified an embedding and vector-search flow that finds the most relevant approved content before the model writes a response. The system prompt requires an evidence-based answer or an explicit escalation.

Add supported actions

Designed tool-assisted workflows for tasks such as ticket creation and status lookup, with intent checks and clear handoff behavior for cases that required a person.

Evaluate the product

Validated retrieval relevance, answer quality, latency, groundedness, and escalation behavior. These measures connected technical performance with whether customers received a useful and trustworthy response.
