Reporting and finance

Tableau Usage Dashboards

Launched the first HSG quarterly usage review across 27 workbooks in under two weeks. The team could see which reports people returned to and which needed a closer look.

Tableau Report Library

Built a searchable library for 24 dashboards. Sixteen report owners reviewed the entries before launch, checking links, ownership, and access paths.

OpEx Drill Down

Built a neighborhood-level profit-and-loss view in Anaplan. Finance leaders can trace a variance from the hospital level through six levels of the organization. The monthly data load runs automatically.

Active Hospitals State View

Prototyped a state-level view of 767 hospitals across 43 states. The crosswalk matched 98% of sites, and the pitch included a 30-day pilot.

Neighborhood Report

Built a Python pipeline that reduces a 3,151-row export to 767 centers, with a check for new or moved centers. Held the file for a fresh data feed before delivery.

Book of Business Refresh

Rebuilt four months of updates across seven master files and found 650 missing rows in past data. The refresh and handoff were still in progress.

Clinical and field operations

Hospital Reference Binder

Moved required paper forms into Anaplan for more than 750 hospitals. Field teams could use their existing logins. After five training sessions, the binder went fully electronic in July 2026.

HD 1:1 Pre/Post Treatment Analytics

Rebuilt the matching logic for overnight shifts. Dropped shifts fell from 224 to 21, and a validation check caught a timing error before it appeared in a leadership review.

Premium Pay Compliance

Built a five-dashboard Tableau prototype to review why premium pay was recorded. Checked the model against the requester’s existing leaderboard across 22 editors.

Pyramid Report Uploads

Turned a field complaint into a personal upload checklist for 150 teammates. Tested it with five field users and condensed the job aid into six steps.

Requests and access

HSG Inbox Optimization

Kept email as the front door and gave five analysts a shared queue. Median first response reached 0.9 days, with 214 of 243 requests resolved in Q3.

Daily Alerts and Weekly Digest

Built owner alerts that skip a notification when the owner is already on the email. A weekly digest gives five queue owners one place to review open work.

Acute Center Request AI Agent

Designed a form review against more than 20 rules. The agent drafts the next email with the exact fixes the field needs to make or routes a completed request for account setup.

HSG Access Management

Found that 194 of 607 inbox requests concerned access. Tested the guide with reviewers and new hires, then shipped one shared lookup table for finding the right request path.

Field communication

HSG National Call

Own the agenda and live production for a monthly, hour-long webinar reaching 400 field leaders. Coordinate five or more speakers, run the green room, and maintain a handoff guide for the next producer.
