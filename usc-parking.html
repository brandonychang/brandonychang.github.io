USC · Applied AI product

USC Parking Intelligence

A multimodal retrieval experience that combines traffic documents, live data, maps, and route context to turn a parking question into a usable recommendation.

Role

Product design and applied machine learning

Focus

Parking and route decision support

Methods

FAISS, language models, maps, live data

(01) — Case study

Connect information to a decision

Unify fragmented inputs

Designed a retrieval workflow spanning traffic documents, live conditions, location context, and map information. The system organized several source types around one user question.

Retrieve relevant context

Used FAISS to find the most relevant document passages and paired them with current route and location data before generating a response.

Present an actionable answer

Structured the experience around destination, route, and parking guidance instead of returning a loose collection of search results. The product translated retrieved context into a concise recommendation.

Design for transparency

Kept source context visible in the response flow so users could understand which information shaped the recommendation and judge whether conditions still applied.
