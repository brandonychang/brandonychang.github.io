Tableau Usage Dashboards

One of our most-used finance reports took nearly two minutes to open. A quarterly review of 27 workbooks put that report on the list for a performance fix.

How the quarterly review worked

Start with the workbook

I built five dashboard tabs from four Tableau Cloud usage sources and launched the review in under two weeks. Report owners could compare distinct readers, visits, and return visits across a period of 7 to 90 days. I excluded admins and creators so maintenance activity wouldn’t count as field use.

Follow a reader down to the page

In the October walkthrough, we started with a workbook tagged Underutilized over 90 days. Selecting it filtered all five tabs. Switching the catalogue to individual pages showed which parts people opened and which they skipped.

Choose what to fix

The review tracked 1,948 loads. Comparing demand with load time surfaced the slow finance report. In a broader inventory of 193 workbooks, 80 had gone unvisited for at least 90 days. That gave owners a separate list to check for old and test copies.

Reuse the review

Patient Pathways became the second team to use the template. Adding its 19 workbooks took one day.
