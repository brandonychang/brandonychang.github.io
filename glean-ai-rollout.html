DaVita · Enterprise AI adoption

Enterprise AI Search Rollout

A trust, workflow, and adoption program for enterprise AI search across fragmented healthcare knowledge systems, focused on the gap between receiving access and building a durable work habit.

Role

Rollout effectiveness and product adoption

Focus

Workflow fit, grounded answers, and user trust

Methods

Behavioral analytics, UAT, feedback triage, roadmap

(01) — Case study

Turn access into trusted usage

Diagnose the adoption gap

Separated basic access from meaningful behavior by framing adoption through coverage, activity, and stickiness. Combined usage patterns with feedback and support themes to identify where users abandoned the experience.

Map friction by user group

Distinguished workflow needs across knowledge workers, support teams, and operational field leaders. The analysis showed why a separate destination, generic use cases, and limited mobile access created different adoption barriers for each group.

Make trust measurable

Organized weak experiences into reliability, grounding, knowledge coverage, and workflow-fit issues. This helped the team treat inaccurate answers and unsupported citations as product defects rather than generic resistance to AI.

Prioritize workflow entry points

Focused the roadmap on meeting users inside familiar work patterns, including browser-based entry points, role-specific agents, and connections to existing knowledge and support systems.

Build feedback loops

Used behavioral ratios, structured feedback, support categories, and user acceptance testing to connect product changes with repeat usage. The approach emphasized decision usefulness and trust over raw login counts.

Support change management

Paired product improvements with role-specific demonstrations, peer advocates, office hours, and targeted onboarding so each group saw a concrete workflow rather than a general AI capability.
