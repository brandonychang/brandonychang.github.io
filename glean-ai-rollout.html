DaVita / Enterprise AI adoption

Access is not adoption.

The rollout focused on the gap between receiving an AI search tool and trusting the answer enough to use it again inside a real healthcare workflow.

Four friction themes

Hover to inspect where trust broke.

01

Reliability

Answers failed to feel consistent enough for repeated use.

Product response: capture repeatable defects and validate fixes through UAT.
02

Grounding

Users needed clear support for claims and citations.

Product response: treat unsupported answers as quality failures, not user resistance.
03

Coverage

Fragmented repositories left gaps in what search could retrieve.

Product response: prioritize authoritative knowledge connections by role.
04

Workflow fit

A separate destination added friction to common tasks.

Product response: bring entry points closer to existing browser and support workflows.

Segmented rollout

One AI product, three different jobs.

Knowledge workers

Cross-system research, document retrieval, synthesis

Support teams

Fast policy lookup, grounded answers, case resolution

Field leaders

Mobile access, role-specific guidance, minimal context switching
