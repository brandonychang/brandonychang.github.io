IFF · Fermentation R&D

Cellulosic Biofuel Analysis

An experimental and analytical workflow for measuring cellulosic composition, quantifying monomeric sugars, and interpreting fermentation yield in biofuel and bioethanol research.

Role

Data science intern

Location

Palo Alto, California

Methods

HPLC, Python, Excel, MATLAB, acid hydrolysis

(01) — Case study

Connect laboratory methods with usable data

Refine acid hydrolysis

Developed and refined a severity-based acid hydrolysis procedure to break cellulosic material into monomeric sugars. Adjusted acid concentration, temperature, and reaction time to improve consistency and sugar recovery across experimental runs.

Quantify sugar profiles

Used high-performance liquid chromatography to separate and identify sugars in hydrolysate samples. Built calibration curves for quantitative concentration analysis and applied quality-control checks to protect accuracy and reproducibility.

Automate data extraction

Wrote Python scripts to extract and process experimental results, reducing repetitive manual entry and the risk of transcription errors. The workflow prepared cleaner inputs for downstream analysis.

Visualize fermentation yield

Connected processed data with Excel and MATLAB to graph fermentation-yield metrics and compare conditions. The visualizations helped the R&D team interpret chemical composition, fermentation efficiency, and potential bioethanol yield.

Support process optimization

Combined wet-lab measurements, calibration logic, automated processing, and visual analysis into a repeatable workflow. The project supported ongoing evaluation of cellulosic feedstocks and fermentation conditions.
