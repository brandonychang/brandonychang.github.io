Portfolio goal

Make recurring healthcare decisions faster without asking clinical teams to become data analysts.

The six releases were not versions of the same dashboard. Each started with a different operating problem: unreliable AI answers, low safety-tool adoption, technician turnover, slow reporting, manual quality checks, or an overloaded analytics inbox.

My responsibility
Turn the operating problem into requirements, prioritize the smallest useful release, coordinate delivery, and define how adoption and workflow change would be measured.
What shipped
Six end-to-end products spanning AI search, safety action planning, retention, automated reporting, clinical quality checks, and request operations.

The six releases

Each product solved a different problem for healthcare teams.

01 / Knowledge

Glean AI Knowledge Assistant

Goal: help clinic staff find reliable policy answers without searching several disconnected systems.

Reliability before scale
87% daily active use in three pilot clinics
Cleaned the source library, categorized failure modes, added safe fallback behavior, and shipped prebuilt prompts around common policy questions.
02 / Safety

Safety Action Dashboard

Goal: turn anonymous safety survey results into a clinic discussion, root cause, owner, and next action.

Adoption rose from 35% to more than 80%
Protected anonymity, grouped themes, and embedded action plans so the tool supported clinics instead of ranking them.
03 / People

PCT Retention Journey

Goal: reduce the six-to-nine-month quit spike by making training progress and the next career step visible.

Pilot retention length increased 40%
Connected stay bonuses, training milestones, a visible career ladder, and manager coaching aids.
04 / Reporting

Pyramid Report Modernization

Goal: replace a stale, manually assembled report with one current view leaders could use in operating reviews.

Reporting time fell 80%; 15 hours saved weekly
Mapped 15 manual handoffs, retired low-value metrics, standardized definitions, automated ETL, and added contextual write-back.
05 / Quality

Clinical Requirements Heatmap

Goal: show missing treatment requirements immediately instead of comparing source files by hand.

Four hours became five seconds
Unpivoted source files in Power Query, converted qualitative rules to numeric logic, and highlighted missing requirements immediately.
06 / Operations

Healthcare Analytics Request Tracker

Goal: create a trackable request queue from the inbox without changing how staff submitted work.

EMAILCLASSIFYSLA
About 250 requests structured across 40+ fields
Automated classification, thread deduplication, ownership, business-day SLAs, resolution tracking, and an offline leadership dashboard.

What connects the six

Each launch made a difficult behavior easier.

01

Start with the real problem

People struggled with trust, unclear career paths, slow reports, manual checks, and an overloaded inbox. Each problem needed a different answer.

02

Make the next action clear

I designed around what a nurse, field leader, manager, or analyst needed to do next.

03

Measure what changed

I tracked use, time saved, retention, and reporting speed after launch.
