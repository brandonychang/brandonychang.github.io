01 / Why the inbox mattered

One inbox.
No shared system.

Field teams used one inbox for access issues, reporting questions, data problems, and technical support.

Ownership and follow-up depended on memory. Leaders had little visibility into workload, aging requests, or team capacity.

02 / Before & after

From inbox memory
to a shared queue.

Before / Manual follow-up

Email was the record.

  • No shared backlog.
  • Ownership depended on memory.
  • Aging requests were hard to see.

After / Structured intake

Every request has a record.

  • Emails are captured and grouped by thread.
  • Owner, priority, status, and SLA are tracked.
  • Dashboards and reminders surface open work.

How it works

Automate coordination.
Keep the workflow simple.

  4. 04

    Follow through

    Update status, ownership, and priority as work moves.

  5. 05

    Make visible

    Refresh the dashboard and send owner reminders.

04 / Lessons & next steps

Use the data
to reduce repeat work.

01 / Prevent repeat demand

Fix recurring friction upstream.

Use request patterns to improve access guidance and address recurring technical issues.

02 / Improve status accuracy

Capture resolution consistently.

Improve reply tracking so completed work closes cleanly.

03 / Design for daily habits

Bring work to the team.

Weekly reminders surface open requests without requiring another dashboard check.

04 / Build for continuity

Scale beyond the prototype.

Move the workflow into a durable shared system for broader team use.
